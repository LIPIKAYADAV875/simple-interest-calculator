# Simple Interest Calculator

A simple program that calculates simple interest on a loan or investment.

## Formula
Simple Interest = (P × R × T) / 100

- P = Principal amount
- R = Annual rate of interest (%)
- T = Time in years

## Inputs
- Principal
- Rate of interest
- Time (in years)

## Output
The simple interest and the total amount (Principal + Interest).

## How to Use
1. Run the program.
2. Enter the principal, rate, and time when prompted.
3. The calculator displays the interest and total amount.

## Example
Principal = 1000, Rate = 5, Time = 2
Simple Interest = (1000 × 5 × 2) / 100 = 100
Total Amount = 1100
